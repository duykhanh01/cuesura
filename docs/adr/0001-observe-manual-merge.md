# Observe manual merges to complete tasks

Feature 002 hands PRs over for the project owner to merge manually, possibly after editing the head themselves or while a fixer/effect is still running; per [SPEC.md S01](../../SPEC.md), completion must reflect a confirmed merge. Decision: add a path into the core for receiving merge evidence: durably record a merge to the correct repo/PR/target branch, block new business work, stop the task's processes and reconcile effects already sent before recording DONE; a head different from the checked head still becomes DONE with `merged_head_unverified`, both heads and a statement that the quality of the merged head is not certified, and the dependency condition is satisfied once DONE is recorded. This keeps the truth of the external action and preserves the remaining work per [FR-040](../../specs/002-task-to-green-pr/spec.md); sending the merge ourselves is deferred to a later feature because it is outside the scope of 002, while cancel-only and staying in a wait merely because the head is uncertified were rejected because they do not reflect the merged outcome and do not provide a meaningful way to finish.
